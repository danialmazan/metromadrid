Metro de Madrid demand dashboard

Demand through collapse, recovery and disruption.

This dashboard combines official monthly demand, daily network totals, station entries, annual station utilisations and GTFS geography to explore how the Metro network changed from 2015 to January 2026.

Derived insights

Weekday structure, recovery breadth and comparative rankings

These views are network-wide. The provided spreadsheets include monthly station data and daily network totals, but no time-of-day breakdown, so the insight layer focuses on weekday structure, recovery breadth and station-level comparison metrics.
